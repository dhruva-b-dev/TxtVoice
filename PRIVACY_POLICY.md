# Privacy Policy for TxtVoice

**Last Updated: July 28, 2026**

This privacy policy applies to the **TxtVoice** app (herein referred to as "the Application") for mobile devices, created by **Dhruva** (herein referred to as "the Service Provider") as a Free service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects and uses the following information to provide its core functionality:

*   **Microphone Access**: The Application requires access to your device's microphone to capture voice for transcription. Voice data is processed in real-time.
*   **Voice Data Processing**: 
    *   Voice data is processed locally on the device or via **Google Speech Recognition services**.
    *   The Service Provider **does not store** your voice recordings on any external servers managed by the Service Provider.
    *   The Service Provider **does not share** your voice recordings with third parties, except as required by the speech recognition engine (Google).
*   **Internet Access**: The Application requires internet access to connect to Google's Speech-to-Text services if on-device processing is not available or chosen.

## 2. Third Party Services

The Application uses third-party services that have their own Privacy Policies. These services include:

*   [Google Play Services](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/)
*   [Google Speech Recognition Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider does not retain any personal data or voice recordings. Any data processed during the use of the Application is transient and deleted once the transcription process is complete or the session is ended.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the Service Provider.
